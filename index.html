About Daily Light

A small, quiet corner of the internet.

Why this site exists

Daily Light was built as a small daily ritual — one Bible verse, one line of encouragement, and a minute to breathe. The idea is simple: most days don't need a long devotional or a big commitment, just a short pause before the day picks up speed.

The content changes automatically each day, so there's always something new to sit with, whether you visit once or make it part of your morning.

Where the content comes from

The verses on this site are drawn from the King James Version of the Bible, which is in the public domain. The lines of encouragement are written for this site and aren't attributed to any particular author.

About the breathing exercise and sound

The breathing guide follows a simple box-breathing pattern (in, hold, out, hold) that's commonly used for a quick moment of calm. The background sound isn't a licensed music track — it's generated live in your browser, so it can play without needing to load or store any audio files.

Your data

This site doesn't have user accounts and doesn't collect personal information on a server. A small daily streak count is saved only in your own browser's local storage, and it stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy below for more detail, including information about advertising.

Information we collect directly

This site does not have user accounts, does not run a server-side database, and does not ask you to submit personal information anywhere on the site.

The site does store a small amount of information in your browser's local storage, kept only on your own device:

This information is never sent to us or to any server — it stays in your browser and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
